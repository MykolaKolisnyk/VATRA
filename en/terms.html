Using VATRA

VATRA is a social platform for communication and interaction between people, creators, and businesses. These terms set out the general rules for using the website and your account.

By using VATRA’s available features, you agree to follow these rules and applicable laws. If you do not agree, do not create an account or use features that require sign-in.

Your account

To register, you must provide your first and last name, date of birth, email address, and create a password. Gender is optional. You must be at least 13 years old and meet any stricter age requirements that apply in your country.

Provide accurate information, keep your password secure, and do not share access with others. Impersonating another person, using someone else’s account, or creating accounts to bypass restrictions is prohibited.

Respect and safety

Fraud, spam, harassment, threats, unlawful disclosure of private information, child exploitation, incitement to violence, and illegal content are prohibited on VATRA.

Do not try to access other people’s data, bypass security measures, collect information automatically without permission, or overload the service. Respect other users’ rights and dignity.

Content and copyright

You are responsible for content you post on VATRA and must have the right to publish it. You retain ownership of the photographs, text, videos, and other content you create.

When you publish content using available service features, you grant VATRA only the permission needed to store, technically process, and display it to your chosen audience. This does not transfer ownership of your content to the platform.

Business, goods, and services

If you use VATRA to promote a business, list products, or sell goods or services, describe your offerings accurately, do not mislead buyers, respect consumer rights, and comply with applicable trade laws.

The terms of a particular purchase, refund, delivery, and the parties’ responsibilities depend on the service and the applicable agreement. The VATRA registration form does not accept payments — do not enter payment details into registration fields.

Account security

To protect accounts, VATRA may require email confirmation and apply technical safeguards against abuse. No online service can be guaranteed to be entirely error-free.

If you lose your password, use password recovery. If you suspect that your account has been compromised, never share verification codes or passwords with others.

Restrictions and liability

Violations of these rules may result in restricted access in accordance with applicable laws and procedures. Your mandatory rights, including personal data and consumer protections, remain in effect.

VATRA may update its features and terms. Material changes to these terms will be posted here with an updated date; where required by law, users will be notified separately.

Policies and contact

Information about how we use your data appears in our Privacy Policy, and information about browser storage appears in our Cookie Policy.

VATRA is owned by Mykola Kolisnyk, an individual. For questions about the terms or privacy, email vatra.support@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or personal documents without a dedicated secure process.
