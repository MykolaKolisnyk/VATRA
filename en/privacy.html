About your privacy

This policy explains which data VATRA processes when you visit the website, create an account, sign in, or reset your password. VATRA aims to process only the information needed to provide available service features.

VATRA does not sell registration information to advertisers, and its pages do not include advertising pixels for profiling.

Data processed by VATRA

  • Account: first and last name, date of birth, and email address; gender is optional.
  • Authentication: user identifier, session information, and authentication logs.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser details, and requests received by hosting and authentication servers.
  • Browser data: session information stored to keep you signed in.

You do not need to provide a phone number or payment details through VATRA’s registration form.

Why VATRA needs your data

This information is processed to create accounts, verify sign-ins, support password recovery, prevent unauthorized access, and operate VATRA.

Depending on the data and circumstances, the legal basis for processing may be performance of the Terms of Service, compliance with law, necessary security measures, or your consent.

Who receives technical data

VATRA uses service providers for authentication, website hosting, and delivery of technical resources. These providers may process technical request data, such as your IP address and browser details, when delivering their services. To ask about the providers and data processing arrangements, contact vatra.support@gmail.com.

Your password is transmitted to the authentication service over HTTPS and is not stored in the website’s public HTML or JavaScript. Data transfers and storage may depend on the infrastructure used by each provider.

Storage and deletion

Account data is retained for as long as necessary to maintain your account or comply with legal obligations. You can remove browser session data through your browser settings; signing out of VATRA ends the local session.

Retention periods for technical logs depend on service and provider settings. The interface does not currently provide self-service account deletion, so deleting all account information with one click is not currently available.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to your data, request correction or deletion, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where it is the relevant legal basis, or contact the competent authorities.

In Ukraine, your rights are governed, among other laws, by the Law on Personal Data Protection. The GDPR may also apply to users in the European Economic Area.

Data security

Sign-in uses HTTPS and a secure authentication system. For security, use a unique password, keeping verification codes private, and signing out on shared devices.

No technical system can guarantee zero risk. If you notice suspicious activity, change your password using the available recovery form.

Data controller and updates

VATRA data controller: Mykola Kolisnyk, an individual. The controller determines the purposes and methods of processing personal data in connection with VATRA.

Privacy contact: vatra.support@gmail.com. You can use this address to request access, correction, or deletion of your data, or to ask about privacy. Account ownership may need to be verified before a request can be processed.

Changes to personal data processing will be reflected in updated versions of this policy. Users will be notified of material changes when required by law.
