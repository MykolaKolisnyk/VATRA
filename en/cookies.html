What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data saved by your browser at a website’s request. localStorage is separate browser storage where a website can keep information between visits. These are different technologies, although both may be used for sign-in and settings.

What VATRA uses

TechnologyPurposeUse
Local browser storage (localStorage)Preserving your session so you can stay signed in without repeatedly entering your passwordYes
Service request dataPage delivery, security, and infrastructure diagnosticsBy service providers
VATRA advertising pixelsTracking for personalized advertisingNot enabled in the code
VATRA marketing analyticsMeasuring advertising campaignsNot enabled in the code

The names of technical session keys may change when the authentication system is updated.

Why storage is needed

Necessary storage helps determine whether you are signed in and keeps your session secure. Authentication may not work properly if browser storage is unavailable.

VATRA pages do not currently include advertising pixels or optional marketing cookies that would require an 'Accept all' choice. We do not display a fictitious consent banner.

How to manage data

  • Select 'Sign out' in your account to end your local session.
  • Use your browser’s website data settings to clear VATRA cookies and localStorage.
  • After clearing the data, you may need to enter your email address and password again.

You can also manage permissions for third-party sites and their cookies in your browser settings.

Third-party services

VATRA uses third-party providers to host pages, handle authentication, and deliver technical resources. They may process necessary technical request information under their own policies. Details of personal data processing are explained in our Privacy Policy.

VATRA cannot directly remove cookies set on other domains. Use your browser settings to manage them.

More information

For general information about how personal data is used, read the VATRA Privacy Policy. For questions about cookies and browser data, contact vatra.support@gmail.com. This page will be updated if the storage technologies change.
